life in the camps

MAHAMA REFUGEE CAMP Life in the refugee camp is not easy, but it teaches people strength, patience, and hope. Every day, thousands of refugees wake up early to start their daily activities. Some children go to school, parents look for work, and others help their families at home. Even though the camp may not have everything people need, many families try their best to create a peaceful and happy life.
